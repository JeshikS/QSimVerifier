🔬 Quantum Device Parameters

Configure real quantum device characteristics. Default values represent typical IBM Quantum superconducting devices (2023-2025 era).

T1 (Energy Relaxation): Time for qubit to decay from |1⟩ to |0⟩ state.
Typical Range: 50-200μs (IBM: ~100μs, Google: ~80μs, IonQ: ~10s)
Physics: Longer T1 = better coherence, less decoherence errors
T2 (Phase Coherence): Time for qubit to lose phase information.
Typical Range: 30-150μs (Always T2 ≤ 2×T1, usually T2 ≈ 0.5-0.8×T1)
Physics: Includes T1 effects plus pure dephasing from noise
Single-Qubit Gate Fidelity: Error rate for H, X, Y, Z, rotation gates.
Real-World Average: 0.05-0.2% (IBM: ~0.1%, Google: ~0.15%, IonQ: ~0.01%)
Source: Calibration errors, control pulse imperfections, decoherence during gate
Two-Qubit Gate Fidelity: Error rate for CNOT, CZ, controlled gates.
Real-World Average: 0.5-2% (IBM: ~1%, Google: ~0.6%, IonQ: ~0.2%)
Source: Crosstalk, longer gate times (~100-500ns), complex control sequences
Operating Temperature: Dilution refrigerator base temperature.
Typical Range: 10-20 mK (IBM/Google: ~15mK, trapped ions: ~1μK)
Physics: Thermal excitation probability ∝ exp(-ℏω/kT), affects qubit populations
Neighbor Qubit Interference: Unwanted coupling between adjacent qubits.
Real-World Average: 2-10% (IBM: ~5%, Google: ~3%, varies by architecture)
Source: Capacitive/inductive coupling, frequency crowding, control line crosstalk
